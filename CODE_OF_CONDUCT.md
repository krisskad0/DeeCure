# Code of Conduct

SysDoc follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The full text at that link is our code of conduct.

## In short

- Be kind and patient. Many of our users and contributors are beginners.
- Respect different views and experience levels.
- Give helpful feedback on the work, never attacks on the person.
- No harassment, insults, or unwanted attention, in any form.
- Do not share other people's private information.
